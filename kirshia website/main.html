About us

Meet the Ocean Experts

We combine adventure with science to show you the hidden wonders of the Atlantic coastline. Come see the sea differently.

Explore the Cape's Living Coastline

Aonyx Marine Experiences invites you to explore the hidden wonders of the Table Mountain National Marine Park. We are dedicated to revealing the vibrant ecosystem of the Atlantic Seaboard through guided adventures that blend science with exploration.

From our original night rock pool tours to immersive snorkel safaris in the magical kelp forests, our experiences are led by qualified marine biologists who ensure every tour is safe, educational, and unforgettable. Join us to discover the ocean's secrets and see Cape Town from a refreshing new perspective.

  • Marine experiences with marine biologists
  • Day & Night rock pool walks
  • Original Night Rock Pool Tours in CPT
  • Tidal pool Snorkeling
  • Sea Kayaking (with Cape Kayak Adventures)

Conservation First

We believe in leaving no trace. A portion of every booking supports local marine conservation initiatives in the Table Mountain National Park. We adhere to strict ethical wildlife interaction guidelines.
