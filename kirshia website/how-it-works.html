The flow

From Ideas to High Tide

A simple step-by-step to get you on the rocks (safely).

1

Pick Your Adventure

Choose a Day Walk, Night Walk, or Snorkel Safari based on what you want to discover. Whether it's glowing critters or kelp forests, we have a spot for you.

2

We Check the Tides

We align every tour with tide charts and weather so you get the safest, most colorful conditions. We'll confirm the best time with you.

3

Meet the Team

Come to our Cape Town base, meet your marine bio-guides, and get geared up. We'll introduce you to the equipment and the plan.

4

Get Your Feet Wet

We handle the gear and safety—you just bring the curiosity. We'll carefully enter the intertidal zone together.

5

Make Memories

Our guides help you spot wildlife, frame photos, and see things you'd otherwise miss. It's a hands-on learning experience.

6

Take the Ocean Home

Leave with new facts, great photos, and a fresh love for the coast. Plus, you'll know exactly what to look for next time.

Safety + gear

Everything included

  • Certified guides trained in ocean safety and marine ecology.
  • Briefing on tides, shoreline conditions, and wildlife etiquette.
  • All necessary equipment provided, including wetsuits and snorkel gear.
  • Small-group formats so everyone gets personal guidance.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need experience?

No experience needed. We tailor every tour for beginners and confident explorers alike.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, and a sense of curiosity. We provide the rest.

Are tours weather dependent?

Yes, we monitor conditions and will reschedule if the sea is unsafe.

Where do we meet?

We meet at the Cape Town Waterfront and confirm details after booking.
