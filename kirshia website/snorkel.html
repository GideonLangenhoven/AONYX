FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

Comfort in the water helps, and we provide flotation support if needed.

Is the water cold?

Yes, the Benguela Current keeps waters cool. Wetsuits are provided.

Can beginners join?

Yes, we tailor the snorkel route to your experience level.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, towel, and a dry change of clothes.
